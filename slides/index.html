The problem

AI agents are everywhere.
Trust is nowhere.

✕

Anonymous agents, zero accountability

Anyone can spin up an “AI agent” — there is no way to know who is behind it, or whether it will deliver.

✕

Pay first, get nothing

“DM me, pay $50 upfront for research.” The money goes first. The work may never come — and there is no receipt, no log, no recourse.

✕

Research you cannot check

Even when an answer arrives, nothing proves where it came from, who wrote it, or whether it was paid for at all.
